Tier 01 | Start here

UA Chapter Member

Get involved on campus, meet fellow engineers, and take part in chapter life.

Open to UA students in good standing

Eligibility

Members must be students currently enrolled at The University of Akron.

Requirements

  • Complete UA Hazing Prevention Training through Brightspace
  • Attend at least three GBMs or official chapter events each semester

Benefits

  • Access to local chapter meetings and events
  • Entry-level resume book inclusion
  • Opportunities to attend local company information sessions
What counts as a chapter event?

Professional development events, study tables, community service and outreach, and chapter socials are included in the proposed requirements.

How long am I considered an active member?

Active membership is evaluated at the end of each semester.

Tier 02 | National access

Convention-Eligible Member

Add national NSBE membership to expand your access to opportunities.

$20 national dues | Self-funded

Eligibility

Students interested in becoming part of the National Society of Black Engineers and its broader network.

Requirements

  • Meet requirements for UA Chapter Membership
  • Pay the $20 NSBE National Dues

Benefits

  • Priority placement at the front of the chapter corporate resume book
  • Access to NSBE scholarships and other perks that come with national membership
  • Eligibility to attend convention with the chapter
Who do I speak with if I'm interested in attending alongside the chapter and am prepared to pay?

Members interested in attending the convention alongside the chapter should contact our president at [email protected].

How do I properly pay my $20 NSBE National Dues?

Members can pay their national dues online through NSBE's national website. To ensure a smooth process, please confirm that your account is affiliated with the UA chapter before making the payment. For assistance, contact our membership chair at [email protected].
