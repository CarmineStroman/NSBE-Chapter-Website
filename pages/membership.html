Tier 01 | Start here

UA Chapter Member

Get involved on campus, meet fellow engineers, and take part in chapter life.

Open to everyone

Eligibility

Members must be students currently enrolled at The University of Akron.

Requirements

  • Complete UA Hazing Prevention Training through Brightspace
  • Attend at least three GBMs or official chapter events each semester

Benefits

  • Access to local chapter meetings and events
  • Entry-level resume book inclusion
  • Opportunities to attend local company information sessions
What counts as a chapter event?

Professional development events, study tables, community service and outreach, and chapter socials are included in the proposed requirements.
